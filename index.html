Hello!

My name is Tran Thanh Vu (Vu is the first name, and my name with Vietnamese tones is Trần Thanh Vũ). This is my personal website where I keep track of and update on professional activities. 

For a full bio of me, please view the Academic CV page. 

I am engaged in a variety of activities:

  • Education: I am a lecturer at the Faculty of Foreign Languages at the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
  • Research: I conduct and supervise a wide range of research projects; most of which focus on (the intersection of) teacher education and professional development as well as the sociology of language education.
  • Writing: I am a book author. I also keep a personal blog on Substack where I share thoughts, notes, and essays. 
  • Community Building: I am the founder and manager of TERECONET, a professional learning community for early-career teacher-researchers in education and related fields. Stay updated with our community activities via this Facebook page. 

I also enjoy travelling and very frequently make time to comtemplate the nature's beauty.

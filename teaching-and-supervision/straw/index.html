Course Introduction

Scholarly Thinking, Reading, and Writing offers a foundational exploration into the domain of academic research and publication. Through a step-by-step approach, students will explore the nature and purpose of scholarly inquiry, learn to navigate literature critically, and engage with current debates including the role of AI in research. The course covers all key components of academic writing, emphasising coherence, presentation, appropriate language, and examination of journals. 

This revised Intake 04 follows a practical approach where students have the opportunity to develop 02 complete manuscripts based on both qualitative and quantitative studies.

Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  • Understand the processes, industry, and key issues within scholarly publishing.

  • Develop critical scholarly thinking skills.

  • Write and review two complete scholarly articles.

  • Apply AI tools in the preparation of academic manuscripts.

Admission Requirements (Please read carefully)

You should possess foundational knowledge and skills related to the nature of research, the process of conducting a literature review, and basic quantitative and qualitative methods. Such expertise may be demonstrated through:

  • Independent or formal training courses in research methods
    Participation in research projects that resulted in publication
    Direct mentorship from experienced researchers

Please provide course certificates, confirmation letters from lead investigators or publications when making your application.

Important Note: This course is only suitable for those with little or no experience in scholarly publication. Please make sure you are a target student before making an application.

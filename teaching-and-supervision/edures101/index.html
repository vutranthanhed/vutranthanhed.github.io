EDURES101

An Introduction to Educational Research

Current Intake: INTAKE 15

Time: Wednesday; 06:00PM - 09:00PM; starting from 26/08/2026

Venue: Kien Tre Building, 3/2 Street, Hoa Hung Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

Course Introduction

This introductory course is designed to provide students with a foundational understanding of educational research, including its fundamental theories, concepts, designs, and techniques. It is ideal for postgraduate (MA/MEd) students specialising in English/Applied Linguistics, TESOL, or Educational Studies. Additionally, language teachers without prior experience in educational research will find this course useful.

The course delves into the essence and reasoning behind educational research and covers the most common quantitative and qualitative research designs. Practical exercises are included to reinforce learning.

Upon successful completion of the course, participants will be awarded a certificate of completion.

Course Objectives

Upon completion of this course, learners will be able to:

  • understand the fundamental theories, concepts, designs and techniques of quantitative and qualitative research in the field of education.

  • read and understand the methods employed in an educational study.

  • identify a valid research problem and formulate an well-defined, answerable research question in the field of education.

  • determine the appropriate method(s) to address a research question.

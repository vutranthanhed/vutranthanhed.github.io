An Introduction to Educational Research Methodology

 An Introduction to Educational Research Methodology is designed for
pre-service and in-service teachers, graduate students, and practitioner researchers who are beginning their journey into systematic educational inquiry. The book assumes no prior formal training in research methods;
it builds understanding progressively, from foundational concepts to hands-on analytic procedures.

Writing for Publication in Educational Research

This book is a practice-oriented guide that takes you step by step through the research writing and publication pipeline, from setting up a project to responding to reviewers and sharing
the published article.

Interrogating Contemporary ELT Pedagogy: Critical, Sociocultural, Decolonial, and AI Perspectives

This textbook is designed specifically for Master of Arts (MA) students
in Applied Linguistics, TESOL, and Language Education. Each chapter is
structured to facilitate deep theoretical engagement alongside immediate
contextual reflection.

Click here to download.

Academic Writing for Postgraduate Studies in Language Education and Applied Linguistics

 This textbook is designed as a structured ten-week instructional
guide for postgraduate students, early-career researchers, and candidates
preparing dissertations, research proposals, and peer-reviewed journal articles across language education and applied linguistics disciplines.

Research Projects

Research Interests

My domains of investigation primarily focus on teacher education and professional development, the sociology of language education, and interdisciplinary perspectives on education. I have conducted and supervised projects on the following topics:

  • language teacher identity
  • language teacher agency
  • language teacher adoption of technology
  • language teacher empathy
  • language teacher research
  • language teacher professional learning
  • experiences of novice language teachers during their first teaching years
  • second-career language teachers
  • perceptions of the teaching profession
  • Buddhist perspective on language teaching

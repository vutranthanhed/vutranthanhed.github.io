[Research Workshop] Crafting a Scholarly Argument for Your Paper

Developing a compelling scholarly argument is often one of the most challenging aspects of academic writing. How do you move beyond presenting data to positioning your voice within the wider academic conversation?

Join us for an interactive, hands-on workshop led by Dr Tran Thanh Vu, designed specifically for early-career researchers and TERECONET members looking to sharpen their paper’s core message and structure!

What We Will Explore

  • Understanding Scholarly Argumentation: What makes an argument truly scholarly?
  • Purpose & Value: Why is a clear argument essential for publication success?
  • Developing Your Argument: Practical steps to build a strong argument using data and theoretical frameworks.
